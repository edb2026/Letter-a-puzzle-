Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Lexyoz (the “Website”, lexyoz.com) offers simple, free learning games for children. We care deeply about kids’ privacy. This policy explains what little information we collect and how we use it. It is written to align with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

The short version

We do NOT collect personal information from children.

  • No names, no email addresses, no phone numbers, no accounts.
  • No advertising and no third-party ad trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of any data.

What we DO collect

We use a privacy-friendly analytics tool to understand, in an anonymous and aggregated way, how the games are used so we can improve them. This includes only non-personal information such as:

  • How many visits the site receives;
  • Which games are opened and played (counts only);
  • Approximate region (country/city) and device type;
  • Whether the app is added to a device’s home screen.

This analytics is configured in a strict privacy mode: no cookies are used for tracking, no behavioral profiles are built, no session recordings are made, and IP addresses are not stored. The information cannot be used to identify any individual child.

Local game progress

Some games save your best score or progress in your own browser using “local storage” on your device. This information stays on your device, is not sent to us, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser data.

Third-party services

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts to display text, and uses an anonymous analytics provider as described above. These services may receive basic technical data (such as a device’s IP address) to deliver content; they are not used to identify children or to show ads.

Parents’ rights

Because we do not collect personal information from children, there is no personal data to review or delete. If you have any questions or concerns about your child’s privacy, please contact us and we will be happy to help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
